What causes Sheehan’s syndrome?

  • Sheehan’s syndrome is a condition caused by the excess loss of blood from a woman after a pregnancy (post-partum haemorrrhage, PPH)
  • This loss of blood can be very severe in cases leading to pituitary infarction and hence dysfunction/hypopituitarism Tutorial Questions 6. Why might this patient have developed Sheehan’s syndrome?
  • This may have happened due to the procedure which she underwent during her pregnancy that caused her to lose a lot of blood
  • This excessive blood loss may have led to pituitary infarction, causing hypopituitarism. 7. What treatment does this patient need?
  • She will need hormone replacement therapy of all pituitary hormones. This is:
  • GH → GH injections
  • FSH/LH → gonadotropin injections
  • ACTH → prednisolone/hydrocortisone
  • TSH → Levothyroxine (T4) replacement therapy
