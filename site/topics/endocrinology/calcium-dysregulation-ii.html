Overview

How does parathyroid hormone (PTH) reduce phosphate levels?

Parathyroid hormone (PTH) reduces phosphate levels by inhibiting the sodium-phosphate co-transporter located in the proximal tubular cells, and allowing phosphate to be excreted without reabsorption

What is the major role of parathyroid hormone (PTH) in the body?

To increase serum calcium levels

Describe the effects that parathyroid hormone (PTH) has on the bone.

Parathyroid hormone increases Ca2+ resorption from the bone back into the blood

Describe the effects that parathyroid hormone (PTH) has on the kidney.

  • Parathyroid hormone increases Ca2+ reabsorption into the blood from the kidney
  • Parathyroid hormone increases PO4 3- excretion, unlike vitamin D
  • Parathyroid hormone increases 1-alpha hydroxylase activity

Describe the effects that parathyroid hormone (PTH) has on the gut.

  • Parathyroid hormone acts indirectly on the gut, as it stimulates 1- alpha-hydroxylase activity → increased serum calcitriol
  • This in turn increases calcium and phosphate reabsorption from the gut to the blood

Describe the effects that parathyroid hormone (PTH) has on calcitriol.

  • Parathyroid hormone (PTH) increases the activity of 1-alpha- hydroxylase
  • This enzyme is used to convert 25-OH hydroxycholecalciferol → 1,25-OH hydroxycholecalciferol

Parathyroid Hormone (Pth) Action.

Describe how we can differentiate between primary hyperparathyroidism and hypercalcaemia of malignancy.

  • Primary hyperparathyroidism involves high PTH levels and high serum calcium
  • Hypercalcaemia of malignancy involves low or low-normal PTH levels but high serum calcium — low/low-normal PTH in response to PTH-rP producing excess calcium
  • NOTE: If a patient with hypercalcaemia has raised PTH, the diagnosis is hyperparathyroidism

Describe how we can differentiate between primary and tertiary hyperparathyroidism.

  • Both involve high PTH and high calcium
  • Primary hyperparathyroidism involves normal renal function
  • Tertiary hyperparathyroidism involves chronic renal failure

How can hypercalcaemia of malignancy be treated?

  • Bisphosphonates - This drug inhibits osteoclasts and stops them from absorbing bone. This in turn lowers calcium and reduces bony pain
  • Rehydrate the patient to try and balance the effects of hypercalcaemia.
