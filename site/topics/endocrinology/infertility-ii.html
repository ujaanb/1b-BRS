Overview

Recall three treatments for hypothalamic amenorrhoea.

  • Reduce training workload
  • Try to avoid stress
  • Increase dietary calorific intake
  • Gonadotrophin injections

Recall three treatments for polycystic ovarian syndrome (PCOS).

  • Lose weight
  • Aromatase inhibitor
  • Metformin
  • Oral contraceptive
  • Insulin management — i.e metformin

Recall a treatment for primary ovarian insufficiency.

  • Hormone replacement therapy (HRT)
  • IVF.

Recall a treatment for hyperprolactinaemia.

  • Dopamine agonist (cabergoline)
  • Surgical removal of pituitary gland
  • IMAGING: Pituitary MRI to visualise potential pituitary adenoma
