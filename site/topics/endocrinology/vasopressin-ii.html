Overview

Recall a test that can be used to diagnose diabetes mellitus.

Blood glucose - HbA1c levels

What should sodium levels be like in psychogenic polydipsia?

  • Low (or normal)
  • This is because there is no underlying physiological dysfunction, the only problem is that there is too much water being consumed. This decreases the osmolality of the blood plasma and hence there is a decreased sodium concentration

Why do we monitor weight during the water-deprivation test?

If weight goes below 97% of the starting weight, the test is stopped as it becomes unsafe for the patient.

How is AVP-deficiency treated?

Desmopressin (AVP replacement therapy)
