When taking a history from a patient presenting with abdominal pain, what 8 salient points about the pain will help you make the correct diagnosis?

  • Radiation

  • Timing

Intra-Abdominal Organs.

What is the meaning of the term “colicky”?

The term colicky refers to a type of pain that is severe, intermittent, and cramp-like, often occurring in waves

What is the meaning of the term “constant”?

The term "constant" when referring to pain describes pain that is persistent, ongoing, and unrelenting over a period of time, without significant fluctuations or periods of complete relief

Describe how we can predict which organs will be associated with colicky and constant pain.

  • Most tubular structures will be associated with colicky pain due to the way they are used
  • The peristaltic movements and passage of substances within the tubes are usually what initiates the pain

When the capsules of solid organs get stretched (i.e organomegaly), this is when there is constant pain

Describe the difference between small bowel obstruction and constipation.

  • Small Bowel Obstruction : A mechanical or functional blockage of the small intestine that prevents normal passage of contents
  • Constipation : Infrequent or difficult passage of stools, typically due to slowed colonic transit or hard stools, without a mechanical blockage

What could central abdominal pain radiating to the back indicate?

Abdominal aortic aneurysm (AAA)

Recall where appendicitis pain refers from.

Central, then right iliac fossa

Describe the difference in clinical presentation between kidney stones and ureteric stones.

  • Kidney stones should present as constant pain because it is a solid organ
  • Ureteric stones should present as colicky pain due to the tubular structure of the organ
