Overview

What treatments can be given for cluster headaches? Acute

  • Triptans — Nasal or subcutaneous route, NOT ORAL
  • High flow oxygen Chronic
  • Verapamil (Calcium channel inhibitor) — Get an ECG first to assess for any pre-existing cardiovascular conditions!
  • Greater occipital nerve block

Why is oxygen given to treat cluster headaches?

Oxygen inhibits neuronal activation in the trigeminocervical system (TGVS)

Describe the NICE guidelines for treatment of cluster headaches.

NICE says to NOT not offer paracetamol, NSAIDS, oral triptans, ergots or opioids as there is no evidence to suggest that they would have any clinical benefit in the treatment of cluster headache — Episodic triptan and oxygen.
